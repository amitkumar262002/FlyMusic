## 🎯 HOW IT WORKS:

### **Opening the Player:**
1. Play any song from Home, Search, or Favorites
2. Tap the mini player at bottom
3. Full player screen opens

### **Using Settings:**
1. Scroll down in the player
2. Tap **"Show Playback Settings"**
3. Settings panel expands
4. Toggle switches or tap items with arrows
5. Tap **"Hide Settings"** to collapse

### **Accessing Equalizer:**
1. Open player settings
2. Tap **"Equalizer"** (has → arrow)
3. Full equalizer screen opens
4. Adjust 16 presets, 5 bands, bass, virtualizer, reverb
5. Tap back to return to player

### **Setting Sleep Timer:**
1. Open player settings
2. Tap **"Sleep Timer"** (has → arrow)
3. Dialog opens with options
4. Select duration (15, 30, 45, 60, 90, 120 min)
5. Music stops after timer expires

### **Changing Audio Quality:**
1. Open player settings
2. Tap **"Audio Quality"** (has → arrow)
3. Dialog opens with options
4. Select: Normal, High, or Extreme
5. Quality changes immediately
